About

I'm a nutraceutical scientist and discovery lead based in Lubbock, Texas. At NemaLife Inc., I help brands in botanicals, biotics, vitamins, and functional ingredients uncover the science behind their products — and turn that science into compelling, defensible market positioning.

With 55+ client projects delivered, I've built BioSeq, an AI-powered discovery pipeline that translates preclinical transcriptomic data into human-relevant health insights — identifying critical genes, biological pathways, and mechanisms of action that open new product avenues for our clients. One discovery uncovered novel health benefits of a century-old biotic ingredient, creating an entirely new positioning opportunity.

My foundation is a PhD in Quantitative Biology (UT Arlington), where I studied mitochondrial stress responses during pathogenic assault. Published in Nature Aging, PLOS Pathogens, and JBC, I bring deep scientific credibility to every client engagement — and the critical thinking to translate complexity into clarity.
